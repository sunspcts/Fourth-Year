    \section*{Congruences modulo a prime power}
    To solve a congruence of the form:

    \begin{equation*}
    ax \equiv b \mod p^l,
    \end{equation*}

    it suffices to solve multiple congruences mod $p$.

    In general:
    \begin{equation*}
    ax \equiv b \mod p^l \implies ax \equiv b \mod p \implies x \equiv x_0 \mod p
    \end{equation*}
