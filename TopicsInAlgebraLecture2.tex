    Given $N \trianglelefteq G$, what is the relationship between subgroups of $G$ and subgroups of $G / N$?

    \centering
    \begin{tikzcd}[row sep=small]
    & G \arrow[ld] \arrow[dd, "\trianglelefteq"] \\
    H \arrow[rd, "\trianglelefteq"] & \\
    & N
    \end{tikzcd}

    \raggedright

    We claim that $H / N < G / N$.

    Proof: $\gamma_N : G \to G / N$ is defined by $\gamma_N(g) = gN$, we call this the canonical map. Using the fact that $\gamma_N$ is a homomorphism, we have that
    \begin{equation*}
        \gamma_N(H) < G/N \iff H / N < G / N
    \end{equation*}

    (Exercise: if $H$ is normal in $G$, prove $H/N \trianglelefteq G/N$).
